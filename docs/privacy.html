Letterwing

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Letterwing is a desktop email and calendar app. It runs entirely on your own computer. There is no Letterwing server, no Letterwing account and no analytics: the people who make Letterwing never receive your email, calendar, contacts or any information about how you use the app.

What Letterwing accesses

When you add an account, Letterwing connects directly from your computer to that account's provider:

  • Google accounts: Gmail (to read, organise, send and draft your email) and Google Calendar (to show, create and answer events).
  • Microsoft accounts: your mail and calendar through Microsoft Graph, for the same purposes.
  • Other accounts: your mail server (IMAP and SMTP) and, if you set one up, your CalDAV calendar server.

It only does what you ask it to: showing your messages and events, sending what you write, and making the changes you make.

Where your data is kept

  • Copies of your messages, events and folder lists are cached in the app's data folder on your computer, so the app is fast and works offline.
  • Sign-in tokens and passwords are encrypted by your operating system's keychain (Windows Credential protection, macOS Keychain, or KWallet / GNOME Keyring on Linux). Letterwing never sees your Google or Microsoft password; you sign in in your own web browser.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere except to your own email and calendar providers, with three small exceptions you can see below.

Other connections the app makes

  • When you add an account, Letterwing looks up your email domain's mail (MX) records in DNS to tell whether it's hosted by Google or Microsoft.
  • Images inside an email that are hosted elsewhere load only when you click "Load images" (or turn that on in Settings), as in any mail app.
  • Calendar subscriptions you add (for example public holidays) are downloaded from the link you gave.

Sharing

Letterwing does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It does not use your data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google user data

Letterwing's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Gmail and Google Calendar is used only to provide the email and calendar features you see in the app, on your own device.

Updates

To tell you about new versions, Letterwing checks the public GitHub releases page from time to time. That request contains no personal information. You can turn it off in Settings → General.

Removing your data

  • Removing an account in Settings → Accounts deletes its cached data and its stored sign-in from your computer.
  • You can also revoke Letterwing's access at any time: Google account permissions or Microsoft My Apps.
  • Uninstalling Letterwing and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.
