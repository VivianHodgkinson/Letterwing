Your mail and calendar,
the way you want them.

Letterwing is a fast, private desktop app for Microsoft 365, Google and any IMAP account. Familiar keyboard shortcuts out of the box, and you can change everything about how it looks and works.

⤓ Download View source

Free and open source (MIT) · Windows, macOS and Linux

Everything you need from a mail app

Plus the things you wish yours did.

Sign in, done

Type your address and sign in with Microsoft or Google in your browser, two-step verification included. Letterwing never sees your password.

All your accounts, one inbox

Work and personal side by side, colour-coded, with a unified inbox, conversations and instant search across everything, even offline.

Your shortcuts

Ctrl+R to reply, Ctrl+Shift+R to reply all, Ctrl+Q to mark read, F9 to send and receive... or switch to single-key shortcuts, or rebind anything, including key sequences.

Calendar and invites

Day, work week, week and month views. Accept or decline invitations straight from the email, see clashes, join Teams calls in one click.

Private by default

Your mail stays on your computer and goes only to your own mail provider. Tracking images are blocked until you choose to load them.

Make it yours

Themes, colours, density and layout: every colour is a setting. Reading pane on the right, at the bottom, or off.

Write like you're used to

Replies quote the original in the familiar business format, signatures per account (paste your existing one, logo and all), drafts saved to your server, attachments by drag and drop, and address suggestions from the people you actually email.

A calendar that keeps up

Microsoft 365, Google and any CalDAV calendar (iCloud, Fastmail, Nextcloud), plus subscriptions such as public holidays. Reminders keep coming with the window closed: Letterwing waits in the tray.

Download

Get the latest version from the releases page. Letterwing updates itself (or tells you when there's a new version, for system packages).

System File
Windows 10 / 11 Letterwing-…-setup-x64.exe (or arm64). A portable .exe that needs no install is also there.
macOS Letterwing-…-mac-arm64.dmg for Apple silicon, x64 for Intel Macs.
Fedora, RHEL, openSUSE .rpm: sudo dnf install ./Letterwing-…-x86_64.rpm
Ubuntu, Debian, Mint .deb: sudo apt install ./Letterwing-…-amd64.deb
Arch, Manjaro .pacman: sudo pacman -U ./Letterwing-…-x64.pacman
Any Linux .AppImage (updates itself) or .tar.gz

The builds aren't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (click More info → Run anyway) and macOS may say it can't check the app (right-click it in Applications and choose Open the first time).
